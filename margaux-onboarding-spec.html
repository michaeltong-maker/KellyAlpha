A short walk with Margaux

An in-chat host who greets every new desk owner, walks her through the building one stride at a time, and stays pinned afterward for whenever she loses her way. Not a modal that flashes and vanishes — a conversation that keeps pace with the user.

Front-desk persona · in-chat coached thread · for the AlphaWalk UI rebuild

The idea

Onboarding as hospitality, not instruction

AlphaWalk hires out analysts who walk a beat and file reports. So the first-run experience shouldn't read like a product tour — it should feel like arriving at a well-run editorial house and being met at the front desk by someone who knows where everything lives. The existing brand promise is calm confidence, peace of mind the moment the app opens. A warm, unhurried human host delivers that better than a stack of tooltips ever could.

The metaphor is consistent end to end: the user commands a desk; analysts are columnists who walk a beat and file; onboarding steps are strides (the brand's core graphic — alpha drawn as a rising stride). Margaux is the house's front desk: she handles the building, never the markets — that distinction keeps her scope clean and quietly reinforces that real analysis comes from the columnists you hire.

The guide

Meet Margaux

M
Margaux
The Front Desk

She runs the front desk at AlphaWalk — the first voice you meet and the one you return to when you're unsure where something lives. She doesn't file market reports; she onboards you, points you to the right columnist, and otherwise stays out of the way. The name leans into the fashion-house register the brand borrows: elegant, distinctive, a little luxe — a concierge, not a chatbot.

Voice principles

Unhurried & precise

Short, clean sentences. She never rushes or hypes. Confidence shows through restraint — the same way the interface earns calm by leaving things out.

Newsroom metaphors

Walk, beat, file, desk, dossier, column, floor. The language reinforces what the product actually is, so the metaphor teaches the model.

Warm, lightly candid

She'll say a thing plainly — including that Create is rough. A host who's honest with you is a host you trust. No exclamation marks, no emoji.

Stays in her lane

"I handle the building, not the markets." She routes market questions to the columnists, which protects the product's core promise and her credibility.

Visual treatment

Margaux uses the columnist monogram system, but where market analysts get a muted author-ink colourway, she carries the brand's ink-violet accent gradient — marking her as the house voice, not a beat. Pinned to the top of the Chat list with a small accent dot and a Front Desk kicker in place of a market beat. No Reports List, no Profile tab — she's a thread, not an agent you hire.

The journey

Four strides, then she stays

Progress is shown with the stride motif rather than "Tips 1/4" — onboarding steps are literally strides. Each stride is one message from Margaux plus a single button that walks the user to the relevant surface. She advances as the user actually does the thing, so the thread reads like a real conversation.

Why she persists

Most onboarding evaporates the instant it ends, taking its goodwill with it. Margaux stays pinned as a quiet standing thread, so the help the user trusted on day one is still there on day thirty. This is the payoff of putting onboarding in the chat rather than in a modal — the channel outlives the tour.

The complete journey

Contextual nudges — every step she'll encounter

Beyond the four strides, Margaux's voice surfaces at the moments a new user is most likely to stall. Each is one quiet line, dismissible, never repeated — they make the onboarding a journey across the whole product rather than a one-time carousel.

Empty state · My Agents, no hires yet
"Your desk is quiet — no analysts on it yet. Want me to walk you back to the floor?"
Turns a dead-end empty screen into a one-tap route back to the Marketplace, in her voice. Button: Back to the floor →
After the first hire · calm, no celebration
"Good pick — Hang Seng Sentiment is filing your first report now. I'll let you read it in peace; you'll find it under My Agents in a moment."
The brand spec says hiring is one-time with no confetti. Margaux acknowledges the milestone warmly without breaking the calm, and tells the user exactly where the payoff will appear.
First time a Report Chat opens · inline coachmark
"This is the analyst's own thread — ask it anything about this filing. I stay out of market talk; this is its beat, not mine."
Reinforces the existing "This agent only discusses its own reports" line, and cleanly hands authority from the host to the columnist. Shown once, then dismissed forever.
Return visit · ties to the existing "while you were away" Desk lead
"Welcome back. Three filings landed while you were away — the freshest is up top."
Reuses the persisted lastVisitAt + new-report count the Desk already tracks, voiced by Margaux so returns feel hosted, not transactional.
First entry to Create · progressive disclosure unlock
"Fair warning — Create is still v0.0001. If you like a challenge, by all means try it; just know it can look very complicated in there. The workshop's open, but the floorboards creak — save often, and don't say I didn't tell you."
Create stays hidden until the user has hired or created (existing behaviour). The first time it appears, Margaux sets expectations with a wink — consistent with Stride 4, so the honesty reads as a running joke between friends.

How it works

Mechanics & state

  • Pinned pseudo-thread. Margaux sits at the very top of the Chat list, above hired analysts, with the accent monogram and a Front Desk kicker instead of a market beat. She has no Reports List or Profile — opening her shows only the conversation.
  • Scripted, paced conversation. The walk is a fixed sequence of Margaux bubbles, each with one CTA (reusing .btn-accent and the preset-pill styling). Progress renders as the stride motif with "Stride n of 4."
  • Advances with the user, not ahead of her. Tapping a CTA both navigates and marks the stride seen; the surface action (visit Marketplace, hire, open a report) unlocks the next bubble, so the thread keeps pace. Go off-script and she simply waits — returning shows a soft "Pick up where we left off?"
  • Relationship to the existing first-run modal. Keep the current brand + "what do you follow?" interests modal as the door (it tailors the Marketplace). On landing, Margaux's thread is pinned with one unread inviting the walk. The modal is the door; Margaux is the host inside — no duplication of content.
  • Persisted state. Add a margaux object beside the existing hasSeenWelcome / interests / lastVisitAt: { walkStep: 0–4, walkStatus: 'invited' | 'walking' | 'done' | 'skipped', dismissedNudges: string[] }.
  • Never nags. Skipping collapses the walk and quiets the thread; nudges fire at most once each and respect dismissedNudges. Nothing auto-reopens.
  • Motion & a11y. Bubbles use the existing dossier-rise / ease-out-expo, gated behind prefers-reduced-motion. Every CTA is a real <button> with an aria-label; the thread is keyboard-navigable.
  • Language parity. Every string lives in the i18n dictionary with en and Traditional-Chinese zh values (premium register), per the language policy — no screen mixes the two.
